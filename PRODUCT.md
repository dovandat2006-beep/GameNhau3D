# Quán Nhậu Đầu Hẻm
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User preference: TypeScript, React, Three.js, Node.js, Socket.IO and LiveKit. Single authoritative Node process for v1; deployment target remains open.
## Users
Vietnamese-speaking friends meeting in a browser on desktop and mobile.
## Product Purpose
A real multiplayer third-person 3D sidewalk tavern: walking, seating, text and table voice, shared food and a wholesome question game.
## Capabilities and Constraints
100 reserved or connected sessions per independent room; 17 tables with six seats; server authority; limited reconnect grace; table-scoped voice. No accounts, wagering, payment reconciliation or persistent rooms. Real connection counts only. No claims of verified 100-device capacity without evidence.
## Brand Commitments
Name and all UI in Vietnamese. Rounded chibi characters with articulated natural gestures and realistic environment materials; Vietnamese streets circa 1990–2000; faded ochre walls, plastic stools, warm lamps and a deep blue evening sky. Game world fills the viewport; compact controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, focus-managed dialogs, readable contrast, touch controls, reduced motion and low graphics settings. Virtual beverages only; no pressure to drink alcohol.

Stage dance-offs use consent and cosmetic spectator voting; no gambling or requirement to drink. All counts/results remain server authoritative.

Ship eight presets, six faces, in-room editing and 3D preview. Receptionist is explicitly a scripted NPC, separate from human/session counts.
